Privacy Policy

Last Updated: February 19, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how your personal information is handled in the Apex Wizard mobile application (the "App").

1. Data Collection and Usage

Apex Wizard is designed with privacy as a core principle.

  • No Personal Data Collection: The App does not collect, transmit, or store any personally identifiable information (PII) such as your name, email address, location, or contact details.
  • Local Storage: All information entered into the App—including motorcycle profiles, suspension setups, track session logs, and maintenance data—is stored locally on your device. We (the developers) have no access to this data.
  • iCloud Sync: If you have iCloud enabled on your device, your data may be synced across your own devices via Apple’s secure iCloud service. This data is encrypted and managed by Apple; we do not have access to your private iCloud storage.

2. Third-Party Services

The App does not use any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services. There are no hidden trackers or telemetry systems included in the software.

3. Permissions

The App may request the following permissions for specific features:

  • Notifications: Used only to provide maintenance reminders and service alerts that you have specifically scheduled.
  • Camera/Photos: Used only if you choose to upload a photo of your motorcycle to your local profile.

4. Data Deletion

Since all data is stored locally on your device, you have full control over it. You can delete your data at any time by:

  • Deleting specific bikes or sessions within the App.
  • Uninstalling the App, which will remove all locally stored data.

5. Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.
