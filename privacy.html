Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 18, 2026

Apex Wizard ("we," "our," or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how your information is collected, used, and protected when you use the Apex Wizard mobile application (the "Service").

1. Information We Collect

Apex Wizard is designed with a privacy-first architecture. The core functionality of logging your motorcycle suspension setups, track times, and bike telematics happens directly on your device.

  • Local Data Storage: The bikes you create, setups you log, and track sessions you record are strictly saved locally to your device's internal storage (via SQLite/CoreData/SharedPreferences).
  • Backup Data: If you enable iCloud or Google Drive backups, your encrypted database is synced according to Apple and Google's respective cloud privacy guidelines. We do not have access to these backups.
  • Crash Reports: We may collect anonymous crash reports to help identify software bugs and improve app stability. This data does not contain any personally identifiable information (PII).

2. Data Usage

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data. We do not use your motorcycle telemetry data for advertising. The data you input into Apex Wizard belongs entirely to you and is used solely to provide the Troubleshooter's diagnostic algorithms and your personal Performance Analytics within the app.

3. Third-Party Services

Apex Wizard may contain links to third-party websites or services that are not owned or controlled by us. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those external services.

4. Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page. Changes are effective immediately upon posting.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions or suggestions about our Privacy Policy, do not hesitate to contact us at adrian.dokoza@icloud.com.
