Apex Wizard replaces the chaos of paper notes and guesswork with a structured, professional engineering suite designed for the modern rider.

🛠️

Setup Troubleshooter

Stop guessing which clicker to turn. Our proprietary diagnostic engine categorizes handling symptoms by corner phase: Braking, Turn-in, Mid-corner, and Acceleration. Simply describe what the bike is doing, and the Wizard calculates a probabilistic diagnosis, ranking the most likely suspension or geometry causes. It provides clear, step-by-step mechanical fixes that respect your bike's physical adjustment limits.

Corner Phase Logic Fix-Logic Engine
🏍️

Garage

Apex Wizard adapts to you. Set up your bike's specific adjustment ranges—Clicks, 1/4 Turns, or Millimeters. The app intelligently detects hardware capabilities, showing only the settings your bike actually has (like High-Speed Damping or Electronic Slide Control). Organize unlimited setup variations into Street and Track folders for instant retrieval.

⏱️

Track Log

Build a comprehensive history of every lap you've ever turned. Track Day logs don't just store times; they sync your session data with the specific setup and tire set you were using. Monitor your **Tire Pressure Delta** (Cold vs. Hot) to ensure you're always operating in the grip sweet spot.

📈

Stats

Data without insight is just noise. Our analytics module performs "Head-to-Head" comparisons between different suspension setups. See which clicker changes actually translated into faster average lap times versus one-hit wonders. Track your seasonal progression with high-fidelity charts.

⛽

Fuel Manager

Weight is the enemy of agility. Every extra liter of fuel is roughly 1kg of top-heavy mass. Our Fuel Manager calculates the precise amount of fuel needed for a specific session based on your bike's burn rate and a customizable safety buffer.

⚙️

Service Hub

Motorcycle maintenance isn't just about the odometer. Apex Wizard tracks part health (Oil, Pads, Chain, Suspension) across three distinct metrics: **Calendar Time, Distance, and Track Usage Count**. Visual health bars warn you when a component is nearing its end of life.

🏁

Tire Manager

Track the life of your front and rear tires independently with our dedicated manager. The app automatically calculates mileage by syncing with your bike's odometer delta. Know exactly many sessions and heat cycles a set has survived before that crucial race.
