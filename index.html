Apex Wizard replaces the chaos of paper notes and guesswork with a streamlined, professional engineering suite designed for the modern rider.

🛠️

Setup Troubleshooter

Stop guessing which clicker to turn. Our proprietary diagnostic engine categorizes handling symptoms by corner phase (Braking, Turn-in, Mid-corner, Acceleration). It calculates a probabilistic diagnosis and provides clear, step-by-step mechanical fixes that respect your specific bike's limits.

🏁

Tire Manager

Track the life of your front and rear tires independently. The app computes mileage syncing with your bike's odometer. Instantly cross-reference our database for Hot and Cold targets.

🏍️

Garage

Intelligently detect hardware capabilities based on your active bike. Organize setups into Street and Track folders instantly.

⚙️

Service Hub

Monitor component lifespans automatically across distance, calendar time, and track usage.

⏱️

Track Log

Build a comprehensive history of every lap you've ever turned. Sync session data with your active setup and specific tires. Monitor pressure deltas to ensure you operate in the grip sweet spot.

📈

Analytics & Fuel Manager

Perform Head-to-Head comparisons between different suspension setups and spot your fastest records. Calculate the precise fuel volume required to lighten the bike and slice your lap times without running dry.
